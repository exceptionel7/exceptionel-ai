Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Exceptionel AI ("we", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: your email, password (stored hashed), and brand name.
  • Content you provide: product catalogs, brand settings, and prompts used to generate content.
  • Leads: information visitors share with your chatbot (such as email, stated need, and budget).
  • Connected accounts: access tokens for platforms you connect (e.g., TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), used only to perform actions you request.
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe; we do not store your full card details.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate the Service: generating content, running your sales chatbot, publishing videos to accounts you connect, processing subscriptions, and improving reliability and support.

3. Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers to deliver features, including Anthropic (AI text generation), Runway (video generation), Stripe (payments), Supabase (database hosting), and the social platforms you connect (TikTok, Meta/Instagram/Facebook). Each processes data under its own policies.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the providers above as needed to operate the Service, or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as hashed passwords, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and scoped access tokens. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data, and disconnect any linked social account at any time from your dashboard.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through exceptionel.com.
